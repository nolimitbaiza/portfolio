AI Grid Trader

An AI agent that trades a grid-scale battery on real wholesale electricity prices. It buys when power is cheap and plentiful, sells when it's scarce, and counts battery wear as a real cost so the battery lasts.

Open the project Read the case study
Markets
California · Germany · South Australia
Objective
Maximise profit net of battery wear
Method
Markov decision process, solved exactly with dynamic programming and re-planned every hour
Stack

Pitwall: F1 Constructor Performance Tracker

Every Formula 1 constructors' championship since the first one in 1958, in one dashboard. A Python pipeline loads official results into PostgreSQL, an Express API serves them, and the dashboard compares teams across eras by their share of the points, since a win has been worth anywhere from 8 to 25.
